External-input contradictions requiring controlled resolution The templates are locked as source documents, but the following conflicts must be resolved before production document generation. Issue ID Conflict Effect Required resolution EXT-ISSUE-001 The approved baseline specifies a three-calendar-day installation waiting period. The SOW specifies a three-business-day rescission period and the Maryland notice refers to three days after signing. Installation eligibility and contract cancellation dates may differ. Retain both as separate controls: the contract rescission period governs cancellation rights; the system installation gate must be the later of the approved baseline waiting date and the applicable contract/legal rescission date. Obtain formal legal/business-owner confirmation before release. EXT-ISSUE-002 The SOW says a 50% deposit is due upon signing the Project SOW and after the rescission period. The invoice says the 50% deposit is due upon start of installation. Contract and invoice could state different payment due dates. Select one authoritative payment rule and update either the SOW, invoice, or both before production use. EXT-ISSUE-003 The baseline requires payment verification before project completion. The invoice permits a 50% deposit and a remaining 50% balance at completion. The system must support partial payment and staged verification. Add payment-plan fields for deposit amount, balance amount, due event, payment status, and verification status. EXT-ISSUE-004 The invoice states a 10-year equipment warranty and 1-year work warranty. The SOW states manufacturer equipment warranty and a 1-year GECC labor warranty. The invoice may overstate or simplify warranty obligations. Confirm that the 10-year equipment warranty applies to all equipment or only qualifying equipment, and align the invoice wording with the approved SOW. EXT-ISSUE-005 The Completion Certificate refers to a financing application, funding request, lending partner, and contractor relationship. The SOW describes GECC as the contracting party and financing facilitator. The document set uses different transaction models and defined terms. Confirm whether the certificate is used only for financed projects or for all projects. Add a project financing-type field and conditional document generation. EXT-ISSUE-006 The SOW contains legal remedies, cancellation penalties, title-retention language, and a payment non-dispute acknowledgement. These terms require controlled legal ownership and should not be changed by ordinary users. Store the clauses as controlled template content and require legal/business-owner approval for any edit. EXT-ISSUE-007 The supplied logo PDF contains a Base64 asset, while the templates contain rendered GECC and Maryland license artwork. Reproduction may produce inconsistent image quality or missing artwork. Store the original Base64 asset and extract or obtain standalone Maryland artwork before final production packaging. These issues are not silently resolved by the system. They require formal decisions and must be added to the decision log before the affected templates are marked production-ready. ________________________________________ 4.
