Decision log Issue ID Previous conflicting rules Final rule Reason for decision Decision owner Date approved IR-001 The Master Sales Record was described as requiring approval from the Sales Manager or Comptroller. Another passage allowed the Installation Manager to approve it. The Sales Manager or Comptroller approves the Master Sales Record and all commercial revisions. The Installation Manager approves installation-related operational costs only. Separates commercial authority from installation operations and removes approval ambiguity. GECC Business Owner and Comptroller October 3, 2026 IR-002 Approved records were described as immutable, but technicians were also allowed to update serial numbers, materials, and completion information after approval. Approved commercial fields are immutable. Permitted operational data may be appended through a controlled audit trail. Commercial changes require a new version. Preserves contractual and financial history while allowing installation work to be recorded. Comptroller and Installation Manager October 3, 2026 IR-003 Financial reporting was described as cash-basis, while the system also required accounts receivable, accounts payable, and double-entry accounting. Financial statements use cash-basis reporting. AR and AP are maintained as operational subledgers and management controls. Retains the required reporting basis while preserving operational payment and obligation tracking. Comptroller October 3, 2026 IR-004 Installation was described as occurring three days after customer signing, but the source did not specify whether the period was calendar or business days or which signature started the period. Installation may begin no earlier than three calendar days after the later customer signature on the contract and invoice. Creates one deterministic date calculation for scheduling and system enforcement. Installation Manager and Comptroller October 3, 2026 IR-005 Some workflow descriptions placed GECC signing before customer signing; others required the customer to sign first. The customer signs the contract and invoice first. The authorized GECC signer signs afterward. Establishes a single ordered signature workflow and prevents premature installation. Sales Manager and Comptroller October 3, 2026 IR-006 The Installation Manager was identified as the certificate signer, while another rule allowed the Comptroller to substitute. The Installation Manager signs the completion certificate. The Comptroller may act as substitute when the approved exception reason is recorded. Preserves operational ownership while providing controlled continuity. Installation Manager and Comptroller October 3, 2026 IR-007 Payment evidence requirements differed between cashier’s checks and bank wires, and project completion could be interpreted as possible before verification. Payment evidence must include method, amount, date, project allocation, supporting evidence, and verifying user. Required payment must be verified before completion. Provides an auditable payment-control standard for all payment methods. Comptroller October 3, 2026 IR-008 Lead, Damon, and Jerry amounts were described both as deductions from every project and as payments made only when individuals participated. These amounts are standardized project-level cost allocations. Their monetary values and eligibility rules are controlled configuration inputs. Prevents inconsistent profitability and commission calculations. Comptroller and GECC Business Owner October 3, 2026 IR-009 Fixed compensation and project deductions were sometimes treated as the same financial item. Fixed compensation is recorded separately from project deductions and becomes payable only at the defined completion state. Prevents duplicate costs and separates profitability calculations from contractor obligations. Comptroller October 3, 2026 IR-010 Commission eligibility was described at different points in the workflow, including before and after payment or completion. Commissions are earned only after project completion and are calculated from the approved project version and recorded cost inputs. Aligns commission eligibility with completed and financially controlled work. Sales Manager and Comptroller October 3, 2026 IR-011 Financial calculations could be recalculated from changed source data, potentially changing historical commissions or reports. Commission, profitability, and published-report calculations must retain immutable calculation snapshots. Ensures historical reproducibility and auditability. Comptroller October 3, 2026 IR-012 Published periods were described as both editable and locked. Published financial periods are locked. Corrections require formal adjustment entries. Protects reported financial history while allowing controlled corrections. Comptroller October 3, 2026 IR-013 The source used both “employee” and “contractor” terminology and referred broadly to payroll. “Contractor” is the standard workforce term. “Payroll” is replaced by “contractor payment processing” unless employee support is formally introduced. Establishes consistent legal and operational terminology. GECC Business Owner October 3, 2026 IR-014 Cancelled projects could potentially be reopened or have their identifiers reused. Cancelled project codes cannot be reused. Reopening requires authorization and preserves the complete prior history. Prevents identity collisions and protects audit continuity. Sales Manager and Comptroller October 3, 2026 IR-015 Signature expiry was described inconsistently or not linked to a reissue process. Signature requests expire after 75 days. Renewal or reissue must be authorized and audited. Establishes a clear lifecycle for incomplete signature requests. Sales Manager and Comptroller October 3, 2026 IR-016 The fifth-day reporting requirement did not define the period type or treatment of weekends and holidays. Reports are prepared on the fifth calendar day of the following month. Weekend, holiday, timezone, and late-entry rules are controlled configuration items. Preserves the stated deadline while identifying the remaining operational configuration. Comptroller October 3, 2026 ________________________________________ 2. Traceability rules The following controls apply to future Sprints: • Every new requirement must receive a unique requirement ID. • Every changed requirement must identify the previous requirement version. • Every acceptance criterion must be testable. • Every requirement must map to at least one final-specification section and one Sprint. • A Sprint may not introduce a conflicting role, workflow state, calculation, or terminology without a new decision-log entry. • Superseded requirements remain in the historical traceability record. • Configuration values such as rates, account numbers, templates, and deadlines must be version-controlled separately from policy decisions. ________________________________________ 4.
